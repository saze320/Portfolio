The Outlanders of the 21st Century

The 19th and 20th century saw the emergence of a group of folks whom this current time and age would refer to as old-fashioned with obsolete ideology about the way of life.

Such people, despite the subtle mockrey and pressure from the 21st century have refused to evolved or rather adapt to this fast-paced generation owing to the fact that the young folks of this genration seem to be beside themselves.

This group of old-fashioned folks who I refer to as the OUTLANDERS have remained dogged in keeping with their culture.
